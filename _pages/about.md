I hold a PhD and have Knowledge Transfer Partnership (KTP) experience in human-centric manufacturing systems, focused on digitising operations to improve both operational performance and safety. My work spans manufacturing execution systems (MES) and the applied use of artificial intelligence and IoT sensor technologies to enable smarter, data-driven decision-making on the shop floor.

I am particularly interested in bridging advanced digital technologies with real-world manufacturing challenges to deliver practical, scalable impact.
